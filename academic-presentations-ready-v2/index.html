About

Rodrigo A. Garrido is Professor of Industrial Engineering at Universidad Adolfo Ibáñez in Santiago, Chile. He holds a Ph.D. in Civil Engineering from the University of Texas at Austin and has more than three decades of experience in operations research, transportation, logistics, and academic teaching.

His research draws on stochastic optimization, mathematical programming, network models, and data science to study freight systems, distribution operations, and emergency logistics. His recent work also examines predictability in complex dynamical systems, including cellular automata and urban spatiotemporal processes.

He previously served as Dean of the Faculty of Engineering and Sciences at Universidad Diego Portales (2012–2023) and as Director of the Department of Transportation Engineering and Logistics at Pontificia Universidad Católica de Chile. He was a Distinguished Visiting Scientist at the University of Maryland.
